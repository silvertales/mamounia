Our Story

A legacy of Lebanese hospitality since 1997

Mamounia was born from a deep passion for authentic Lebanese cuisine and a desire to share the rich culinary traditions of Beirut with the world. Every dish is a celebration of fresh ingredients, aromatic spices, and time‑honored family recipes passed down through generations.

Our chefs hand‑pick the finest produce each morning, preparing everything from silky hummus to tender grilled meats with the same care and attention you’d find in a Lebanese grandmother’s kitchen.
